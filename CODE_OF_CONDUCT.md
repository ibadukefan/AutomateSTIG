# Community Code of Conduct

AutomateSTIG contributors and users are expected to keep participation
professional, respectful, and focused on safe cybersecurity engineering.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect regardless of background, identity, experience, or
  role.
- Give specific, evidence-based technical feedback.
- Protect operational and personal information.
- Accept correction, document uncertainty, and avoid overstating security or
  compliance claims.
- Disagree with ideas without attacking people.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, deliberate disclosure of private
information, and knowingly unsafe handling of assessment data are not
acceptable.

## Reporting

Request a private reporting channel from the
[repository owner](https://github.com/ibadukefan) using the contact method on
that profile. If no private method is available, open a detail-free request for
private contact; do not name people or describe the incident publicly. Report
software vulnerabilities through
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/ibadukefan/AutomateSTIG/security/advisories/new),
not a public issue. Reports will be reviewed fairly and with appropriate
confidentiality.

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict
participation when necessary to protect people, the project, or sensitive
data.
